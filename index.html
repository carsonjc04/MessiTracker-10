What this site claims, and what it doesn't

  • Not every game he played. This archive holds the matches StatsBomb has released for free: almost all of his La Liga matches for Barcelona, his two Ligue 1 seasons with PSG, three Champions League finals, the 2018 and 2022 World Cups, the 2024 Copa América and a handful of 2023 Inter Miami league games. Most Champions League, Copa del Rey and Argentina qualifiers and friendlies are absent.
  • A replay, not video. All 22 players are on the pitch, with Messi highlighted. In 106 matches their positions come from StatsBomb's 360 camera frames. In the rest, each player is placed by his own recorded actions and the freeze-frame at every shot. A faint marker means the spot is a guess between two known ones.
  • Nothing is modelled. No expected goals, no ratings, and no movement beyond straight lines between known spots. Counts are of events in the record.
  • One direction. Messi's team always attacks to the right, in both halves, so his work is easy to compare across the match.
  • How we counted. Every definition is written out on What you can see here.
